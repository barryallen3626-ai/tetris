1 Introduction

AGR ASSOCIATES (PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we," "us," or "our") develops and publishes mobile applications available through Google Play.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our applications handle information.

Our applications are currently in development and, at this time, do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

This Privacy Policy explains our current data practices and applies to the applications published by AGR ASSOCIATES (PRIVATE) LIMITED that link to this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

At this time, our applications do not collect personal information or personal and sensitive user data from users.

We do not intentionally collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Account credentials
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Audio or microphone recordings
  • SMS or call information
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health information
  • Authentication information
  • Device identifiers for the purpose of identifying users
  • Personal files or documents
  • Browsing history
  • User-generated content

Our applications also do not maintain user accounts or require users to provide personal information in order to use their current functionality.

3 Data Storage

Our applications currently do not transmit or store user data on our servers or databases.

Any information or settings that an application may temporarily maintain locally on a user's device, where applicable, remain on the device and are not transmitted to AGR ASSOCIATES (PRIVATE) LIMITED.

4 Data Sharing

We currently do not sell, rent, or share users' personal or sensitive information with third parties.

Because our applications currently do not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user data, there is currently no such information to share with third parties.

5 Third-Party Services and SDKs

Our applications may use third-party software libraries or services when required for app functionality, security, distribution, or development.

Before integrating any third-party service that collects or processes user data, we will review its data practices and update this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play Data Safety information as necessary.

We will not intentionally use third-party services to collect or process personal or sensitive user data without appropriate disclosure and compliance with applicable requirements.

6 Permissions

Our applications will only request permissions that are necessary for their intended functionality.

If a future version of an application requires access to personal or sensitive information, we will provide appropriate disclosures and request permissions in accordance with applicable Android and Google Play requirements.

Permissions and data practices may vary between individual applications. Users should review the permissions requested by each application before installation and use.

7 Data Retention and Deletion

Because our applications currently do not collect or store personal or sensitive user data on our systems, we currently have no user data retention or server-side deletion process applicable to such information.

If our applications begin collecting or storing personal data in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain:

  • What information is collected;
  • Why it is collected;
  • How it is used;
  • Where it is stored;
  • How long it is retained;
  • With whom it may be shared; and
  • How users can request deletion where applicable.

8 Children's Privacy

Our applications are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because our current applications do not collect personal or sensitive user data, we do not knowingly collect such information from children.

If an application is specifically directed toward children or families in the future, we will comply with the applicable Google Play Families Policy and other applicable children's privacy requirements.

9 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the applications and our systems against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Since our current applications do not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user data to our servers, we do not currently maintain a database of such user information.

If our data practices change, we will implement appropriate security measures based on the nature of the information being processed.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

If our data collection, storage, processing, or sharing practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy before or when those changes become applicable, as appropriate.

The updated Privacy Policy will be made available at the same publicly accessible URL.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy for the latest information about our privacy practices.
